# HSK 1 Listening — Track 10

Words 271–300. Voices: Sarah (female) and Brian (male).
Chinese word prompts use 请跟我读：… (“Please read after me: …”).
Chinese example sentences are spoken at 85% of the normal speaking rate used in the track.

## 271. 在 — zài

**Full meaning:** to be at; in; at

**Example:** 我朋友在医院工作。

**Translation:** My friend works at a hospital.

## 272. 再见 — zài jiàn

**Full meaning:** goodbye

**Example:** 老师，再见！

**Translation:** Goodbye, teacher!

## 273. 早 — zǎo

**Full meaning:** early

**Example:** 我今天起床很早。

**Translation:** I got up very early today.

## 274. 早饭 — zǎo fàn

**Full meaning:** breakfast

**Example:** 你吃早饭了吗？

**Translation:** Have you eaten breakfast?

## 275. 早上 — zǎo shang

**Full meaning:** early morning

**Example:** 我早上六点起床。

**Translation:** I get up at six in the morning.

## 276. 怎么 — zěn me

**Full meaning:** how

**Example:** 这个字怎么读？

**Translation:** How do you read this character?

## 277. 怎么样 — zěn me yàng

**Full meaning:** how about, what do you think

**Example:** 我们去看电影，怎么样？

**Translation:** How about we go see a movie?

## 278. 找 — zhǎo

**Full meaning:** to look for; to find

**Example:** 我在找我的手机。

**Translation:** I am looking for my phone.

## 279. 这 — zhè

**Full meaning:** this

**Example:** 这是我的房间。

**Translation:** This is my room.

## 280. 这边 — zhè biān

**Full meaning:** this side; here

**Example:** 商店在这边。

**Translation:** The store is over here.

## 281. 这个 — zhè ge

**Full meaning:** this one

**Example:** 我要这个，不要那个。

**Translation:** I want this one, not that one.

## 282. 这里 — zhè lǐ

**Full meaning:** here

**Example:** 这里的菜很好吃。

## 283. 这儿 — zhèr

**Full meaning:** here (colloquial, erhua)

## 284. 这些 — zhè xiē

## 286. 正在 — zhèng zài
