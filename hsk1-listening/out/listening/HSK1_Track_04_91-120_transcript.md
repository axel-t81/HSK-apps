# HSK 1 Listening — Track 04

Words 91–120. Voices: Sarah (female) and Brian (male).
Chinese word prompts use 请跟我读：… (“Please read after me: …”).
Chinese example sentences are spoken at 85% of the normal speaking rate used in the track.

## 91. 叫 — jiào

**Full meaning:** to be called, to call; to make; (by)

**Example:** 你叫什么名字？

**Translation:** What is your name?

## 92. 姐姐 — jiě jie

**Full meaning:** older sister

**Example:** 我姐姐会开车。

**Translation:** My older sister can drive.

## 93. 今年 — jīn nián

**Full meaning:** this year

**Example:** 我今年二十岁。

**Translation:** I am twenty years old this year.

## 94. 今天 — jīn tiān

**Full meaning:** today

**Example:** 我们今天不上课。

**Translation:** We do not have class today.

## 95. 九 — jiǔ

**Full meaning:** nine

**Example:** 现在九点。

**Translation:** It is nine o'clock now.

## 96. 觉得 — jué de

**Full meaning:** to feel, to think

**Example:** 我觉得这家饭店很好。

**Translation:** I think this restaurant is very good.

## 97. 开 — kāi

**Full meaning:** to open; to switch on

**Example:** 请开一下门。

**Translation:** Please open the door.

## 98. 开车 — kāi chē

**Full meaning:** to drive

**Example:** 我爸爸会开车。

**Translation:** My dad can drive.

## 99. 看 — kàn

**Full meaning:** to see; to look at; to watch; to read

**Example:** 我想看那个电影。

**Translation:** I want to watch that movie.

## 100. 看病 — kàn bìng

**Full meaning:** to see a doctor

**Example:** 他生病了，现在在医院看病。

**Translation:** He is sick and is seeing a doctor at the hospital now.

## 101. 看见 — kàn jiàn

**Full meaning:** to see; to catch sight of

**Example:** 我看见老师了。

**Translation:** I saw the teacher.

## 102. 可以 — kě yǐ

**Full meaning:** can, may; okay, fine

**Example:** 我可以坐这儿吗？

**Translation:** May I sit here?

## 103. 课 — kè

**Full meaning:** class, lesson

**Example:** 我们上午有汉语课。

**Translation:** We have a Chinese class in the morning.

## 104. 口 — kǒu

**Full meaning:** mouth; measure word for family members, bites

**Example:** 我们家有四口人。

**Translation:** There are four people in our family.

## 105. 块 — kuài

**Full meaning:** measure word for pieces, chunks, money

**Example:** 这个包子三块钱。

**Translation:** This steamed bun costs three yuan.

## 106. 来 — lái

**Full meaning:** to come

**Example:** 你明天来我家吧。

**Translation:** Come to my home tomorrow.

## 107. 老师 — lǎo shī

**Full meaning:** teacher

**Example:** 我们的老师是中国人。

**Translation:** Our teacher is Chinese.

## 108. 了 — le

**Full meaning:** particle indicating completed action or change of state

**Example:** 我吃了两个包子。

**Translation:** I ate two steamed buns.

## 109. 冷 — lěng

**Full meaning:** cold

**Example:** 今天很冷，你多穿一点儿衣服。

**Translation:** It is very cold today; put on some more clothes.

## 110. 里 — lǐ

**Full meaning:** inside; in

**Example:** 我的书在房间里。

**Translation:** My book is in the room.

## 111. 两 — liǎng

**Full meaning:** two (used before a measure word)

**Example:** 我有两个弟弟。

**Translation:** I have two younger brothers.

## 112. 零 — líng

**Full meaning:** zero

**Example:** 这本书一百零八块。

**Translation:** This book costs 108 yuan.

## 113. 六 — liù

**Full meaning:** six

**Example:** 我星期六不上班。

**Translation:** I do not work on Saturdays.

## 114. 妈妈 — mā ma

**Full meaning:** mom

**Example:** 我妈妈是医生。

**Translation:** My mom is a doctor.

## 115. 吗 — ma

**Full meaning:** question particle

**Example:** 你是学生吗？

**Translation:** Are you a student?

## 116. 买 — mǎi

**Full meaning:** to buy

**Example:** 我想买这件衣服。

**Translation:** I want to buy this piece of clothing.

## 117. 卖 — mài

**Full meaning:** to sell

**Example:** 这家商店卖水果。

**Translation:** This store sells fruit.

## 118. 忙 — máng

**Full meaning:** busy; to be busy

**Example:** 爸爸今天很忙。

**Translation:** Dad is very busy today.

## 119. 猫 — māo

**Full meaning:** cat

**Example:** 我家有一只小猫。

**Translation:** I have a little cat at home.

## 120. 没关系 — méi guān xi

**Full meaning:** never mind, it's okay

**Example:** 没关系，你明天再来吧。

**Translation:** It is okay; come back tomorrow.
