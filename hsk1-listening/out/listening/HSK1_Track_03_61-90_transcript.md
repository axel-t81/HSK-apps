# HSK 1 Listening — Track 03

Words 61–90. Voices: Sarah (female) and Brian (male).
Chinese word prompts use 请跟我读：… (“Please read after me: …”).
Chinese example sentences are spoken at 85% of the normal speaking rate used in the track.

## 61. 给 — gěi

**Full meaning:** to give; (to, for)

**Example:** 我给弟弟一本书。

**Translation:** I give my younger brother a book.

## 62. 公司 — gōng sī

**Full meaning:** company

**Example:** 爸爸在一家公司工作。

**Translation:** Dad works at a company.

## 63. 工作 — gōng zuò

**Full meaning:** work; to work

**Example:** 你明天要工作吗？

**Translation:** Do you have to work tomorrow?

## 64. 狗 — gǒu

**Full meaning:** dog

**Example:** 我们家有一只狗。

**Translation:** We have a dog at home.

## 65. 贵 — guì

**Full meaning:** expensive

**Example:** 这件衣服太贵了。

**Translation:** This piece of clothing is too expensive.

## 66. 国 — guó

**Full meaning:** country

**Example:** 你想去哪个国家？

**Translation:** Which country do you want to go to?

## 67. 还 — hái

**Example:** 我还在工作。

## 68. 孩子 — hái zi

## 69. 汉语 — Hàn yǔ

## 70. 汉字 — Hàn zì
