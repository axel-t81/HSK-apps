# HSK 1 Listening — Track 01

Words 1–30. Voices: Sarah (female) and Brian (male).
Chinese word prompts use 请跟我读：… (“Please read after me: …”).
Chinese example sentences are spoken at 85% of the normal speaking rate used in the track.

## 1. 爱 — ài

**Full meaning:** to love

**Example:** 我爱我的家人。

**Translation:** I love my family.

## 2. 八 — bā

**Full meaning:** eight

**Example:** 我有八本书。

**Translation:** I have eight books.

## 3. 爸爸 — bà ba

**Full meaning:** dad

**Example:** 我爸爸是医生。

**Translation:** My dad is a doctor.

## 4. 吧 — ba

**Full meaning:** particle indicating suggestion, confirmation, or hesitation

**Example:** 我们吃饭吧。

**Translation:** Let's eat.

## 5. 白天 — bái tiān

**Full meaning:** daytime

**Example:** 白天我工作，晚上睡觉。

**Translation:** I work during the day and sleep at night.

## 6. 百 — bǎi

**Full meaning:** hundred

**Example:** 我有一百块钱。

**Translation:** I have one hundred yuan.

## 7. 半 — bàn

**Full meaning:** half; (half, semi-)

**Example:** 现在是九点半。

**Translation:** It's nine thirty now.

## 8. 包子 — bāo zi

**Full meaning:** steamed stuffed bun

**Example:** 我想吃包子。

**Translation:** I want to eat steamed stuffed buns.

## 9. 杯子 — bēi zi

**Full meaning:** cup, glass

**Example:** 这个杯子是我的。

**Translation:** This cup is mine.

## 10. 本 — běn

**Full meaning:** measure word for books

**Example:** 这两本书是我的。

**Translation:** These two books are mine.

## 11. 边 — biān

**Full meaning:** side; suffix indicating direction

**Example:** 我家在学校那边。

**Translation:** My home is over there by the school.

## 12. 病 — bìng

**Full meaning:** illness, disease; to be ill

**Example:** 他病了，今天不上班。

**Translation:** He is ill and is not going to work today.

## 13. 不 — bù

**Full meaning:** not, no

**Example:** 我不是学生。

**Translation:** I am not a student.

## 14. 不客气 — bú kè qi

**Full meaning:** you're welcome

**Example:** 谢谢！不客气。

**Translation:** Thank you! You're welcome.

## 15. 不要 — bú yào

**Full meaning:** don't, not want

**Example:** 我不要这个。

**Translation:** I do not want this one.

## 16. 菜 — cài

**Full meaning:** vegetable, dish

**Example:** 这个菜很好吃。

**Translation:** This dish is delicious.

## 17. 茶 — chá

**Full meaning:** tea

**Example:** 我想喝茶。

**Translation:** I want to drink tea.

## 18. 唱 — chàng

**Full meaning:** to sing

**Example:** 她会唱歌。

**Translation:** She can sing.

## 19. 超市 — chāo shì

**Full meaning:** supermarket

**Example:** 我去超市买东西。

**Translation:** I am going to the supermarket to buy things.

## 20. 车 — chē

**Full meaning:** vehicle, car

**Example:** 这是我的车。

**Translation:** This is my car.

## 21. 吃 — chī

**Full meaning:** to eat

**Example:** 我想吃米饭。

**Translation:** I want to eat cooked rice.

## 22. 出租车 — chū zū chē

**Full meaning:** taxi

**Example:** 我们坐出租车去学校。

**Translation:** We are taking a taxi to school.

## 23. 穿 — chuān

**Full meaning:** to wear

**Example:** 今天她穿新衣服。

**Translation:** She is wearing new clothes today.

## 24. 打电话 — dǎ diàn huà

**Full meaning:** to make a phone call

**Example:** 我在给老师打电话。

**Translation:** I am calling the teacher.

## 25. 大 — dà

**Full meaning:** big

**Example:** 这个大学很大。

**Translation:** This university is very big.

## 26. 大家 — dà jiā

**Full meaning:** everyone

**Example:** 大家今天很高兴。

**Translation:** Everyone is very happy today.

## 27. 大学 — dà xué

**Full meaning:** university

**Example:** 我在大学学习汉语。

**Translation:** I study Chinese at university.

## 28. 大学生 — dà xué shēng

**Full meaning:** university student

**Example:** 我妹妹是大学生。

**Translation:** My younger sister is a university student.

## 29. 到 — dào

**Full meaning:** to arrive

**Example:** 我八点到学校。

**Translation:** I arrive at school at eight.

## 30. 的 — de

**Full meaning:** possessive or modifying particle

**Example:** 他的汉语很好。

**Translation:** His Chinese is very good.
