# HSK 1 Listening — Track 08

Words 211–240. Voices: Sarah (female) and Brian (male).
Chinese word prompts use 请跟我读：… (“Please read after me: …”).
Chinese example sentences are spoken at 85% of the normal speaking rate used in the track.

## 211. 同学 — tóng xué

**Full meaning:** classmate

**Example:** 我的同学都很喜欢汉语。

**Translation:** My classmates all like Chinese.

## 212. 外 — wài

**Full meaning:** outside, exterior

**Example:** 他在车外。

**Translation:** He is outside the car.

## 213. 外边 — wài bian

**Full meaning:** outside

**Example:** 外边很冷，请进来。

**Translation:** It is cold outside; please come in.

## 214. 玩 — wán
