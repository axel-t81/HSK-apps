# HSK 1 Listening — Track 02

Words 31–60. Voices: Sarah (female) and Brian (male).
Chinese word prompts use 请跟我读：… (“Please read after me: …”).
Chinese example sentences are spoken at 85% of the normal speaking rate used in the track.

## 31. 第 — dì

**Full meaning:** prefix for ordinal numbers

**Example:** 今天是十月一号，也是我学汉语的第一天。

**Translation:** Today is October 1, and it is also my first day studying Chinese.

## 32. 弟弟 — dì di

**Full meaning:** younger brother

**Example:** 我弟弟今年七岁。

**Translation:** My younger brother is seven years old this year.

## 33. 点 — diǎn

**Full meaning:** o'clock; point, dot; measure word for small amounts

**Example:** 现在几点？

**Translation:** What time is it now?

## 34. 店 — diàn

**Full meaning:** shop, store

**Example:** 这家店的东西不贵。

**Translation:** The things in this shop are not expensive.

## 35. 电话 — diàn huà

**Full meaning:** telephone, phone call

**Example:** 我晚上给妈妈打电话。

**Translation:** I call my mom in the evening.

## 36. 电脑 — diàn nǎo

**Full meaning:** computer

**Example:** 这是我的电脑。

**Translation:** This is my computer.

## 37. 电视 — diàn shì

**Full meaning:** television

**Example:** 我们晚上看电视。

**Translation:** We watch television in the evening.

## 38. 电影 — diàn yǐng

**Full meaning:** movie, film

**Example:** 这个电影很好看。

**Translation:** This movie is very good.

## 39. 电影院 — diàn yǐng yuàn

**Full meaning:** cinema

**Example:** 我们去电影院看电影。

**Translation:** We are going to the cinema to watch a movie.

## 40. 东西 — dōng xi

**Full meaning:** thing, object

**Example:** 这些东西都是我的。

**Translation:** These things are all mine.

## 41. 都 — dōu

**Full meaning:** all, both

**Example:** 我和弟弟都喜欢猫。

**Translation:** My younger brother and I both like cats.

## 42. 读 — dú

**Full meaning:** to read

**Example:** 我会读这些汉字。

**Translation:** I can read these Chinese characters.

## 43. 读书 — dú shū

**Full meaning:** to read a book

**Example:** 我在房间里读书。

**Translation:** I am reading in my room.

## 44. 对 — duì

**Full meaning:** correct, right; to, toward, for; (to treat; pair)

**Example:** 这个字不对，你再写一下。

**Translation:** This character is not correct; write it again.

## 45. 对不起 — duì bu qǐ

**Full meaning:** sorry

**Example:** 对不起，我来晚了。

**Translation:** Sorry, I am late.

## 46. 多 — duō

**Full meaning:** many, much; how many, how much

**Example:** 这里的人很多。

**Translation:** There are a lot of people here.

## 47. 多少 — duō shao

**Full meaning:** how many, how much

**Example:** 你有多少本书？

**Translation:** How many books do you have?

## 48. 儿子 — ér zi

**Full meaning:** son

**Example:** 她的儿子是小学生。

**Translation:** Her son is a primary school student.

## 49. 二 — èr

**Full meaning:** two (used in counting and numbers)

**Example:** 他今年二十二岁。

## 50. 饭 — fàn

**Full meaning:** cooked rice, meal

**Example:** 我们在饭店吃饭。

## 51. 饭店 — fàn diàn

## 52. 房间 — fáng jiān
