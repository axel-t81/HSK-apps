# HSK 1 Listening — Track 09

Words 241–270. Voices: Sarah (female) and Brian (male).
Chinese word prompts use 请跟我读：… (“Please read after me: …”).
Chinese example sentences are spoken at 85% of the normal speaking rate used in the track.

## 241. 谢谢 — xiè xie

**Full meaning:** to thank

**Example:** 谢谢你，老师。

**Translation:** Thank you, teacher.

## 242. 新 — xīn

**Full meaning:** new

**Example:** 这是我的新书。

**Translation:** This is my new book.

## 243. 星期 — xīng qī

**Full meaning:** week

**Example:** 这个星期我很忙。

**Translation:** I am very busy this week.

## 244. 星期日 — xīng qī rì

**Full meaning:** Sunday (formal)

**Example:** 星期日我不上班。

**Translation:** I do not work on Sunday.

## 245. 星期天 — xīng qī tiān

**Full meaning:** Sunday (colloquial)

**Example:** 星期天我们去看电影。

**Translation:** We are going to see a movie on Sunday.

## 246. 休息 — xiū xi

**Full meaning:** to rest

**Example:** 我今天不工作，在家休息。

**Translation:** I am not working today; I am resting at home.

## 247. 学 — xué
