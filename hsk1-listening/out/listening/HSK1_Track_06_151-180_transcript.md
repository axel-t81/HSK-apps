# HSK 1 Listening — Track 06

Words 151–180. Voices: Sarah (female) and Brian (male).
Chinese word prompts use 请跟我读：… (“Please read after me: …”).
Chinese example sentences are spoken at 85% of the normal speaking rate used in the track.

## 151. 牛奶 — niú nǎi

**Full meaning:** milk

**Example:** 孩子早上喝牛奶。

**Translation:** The child drinks milk in the morning.

## 152. 女 — nǚ

**Full meaning:** female

**Example:** 那个女老师很好。

**Translation:** That female teacher is very nice.

## 153. 女儿 — nǚ ér

**Full meaning:** daughter

**Example:** 我女儿今年七岁。

**Translation:** My daughter is seven years old this year.

## 154. 女朋友 — nǚ péng you
