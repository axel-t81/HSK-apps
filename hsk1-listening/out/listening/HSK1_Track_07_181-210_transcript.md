# HSK 1 Listening — Track 07

Words 181–210. Voices: Sarah (female) and Brian (male).
Chinese word prompts use 请跟我读：… (“Please read after me: …”).
Chinese example sentences are spoken at 85% of the normal speaking rate used in the track.

## 181. 谁 — shéi/shuí

**Full meaning:** who

**Example:** 那个老师是谁？

**Translation:** Who is that teacher?

## 182. 什么 — shén me

**Full meaning:** what

**Example:** 你想吃什么？

**Translation:** What do you want to eat?

## 183. 生病 — shēng bìng

**Full meaning:** to get sick

**Example:** 孩子生病了。

**Translation:** The child is sick.

## 184. 十 — shí

**Full meaning:** ten

**Example:** 我有十本书。

**Translation:** I have ten books.

## 185. 时候 — shí hou

**Full meaning:** moment, time

**Example:** 你什么时候来？

**Translation:** When are you coming?

## 186. 时间 — shí jiān

**Full meaning:** time

**Example:** 你现在有时间吗？

**Translation:** Do you have time now?

## 187. 事 — shì

**Full meaning:** matter, thing

**Example:** 他今天有事，不来上课。

**Translation:** He has something to do today and won't come to class.

## 188. 是 — shì

## 189. 手机 — shǒu jī
