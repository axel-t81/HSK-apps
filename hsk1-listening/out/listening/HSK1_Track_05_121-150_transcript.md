# HSK 1 Listening — Track 05

Words 121–150. Voices: Sarah (female) and Brian (male).
Chinese word prompts use 请跟我读：… (“Please read after me: …”).
Chinese example sentences are spoken at 85% of the normal speaking rate used in the track.

## 121. 没事 — méi shì

**Full meaning:** it's nothing; it doesn't matter

**Example:** 他昨天病了，今天没事了。

**Translation:** He was sick yesterday, but he's fine today.

## 122. 没有 — méi yǒu

**Full meaning:** to not have; have not

**Example:** 我没有时间。

**Translation:** I don't have time.

## 123. 妹妹 — mèi mei

**Full meaning:** younger sister

**Example:** 我妹妹今年八岁。

**Translation:** My younger sister is eight years old this year.

## 124. 们 — men

**Full meaning:** plural suffix for pronouns and nouns

**Example:** 我们都很喜欢中文歌。

**Translation:** We all really like Chinese songs.

## 125. 米饭 — mǐ fàn

## 126. 面包 — miàn bāo
